K2 FITNESS WEBSITE
==================

Project
-------
Website for K2 Fitness, a fitness training center in Yanthampalawa, Kurunegala,
Sri Lanka. Kusal Anjana is the owner, instructor, and fitness trainer.

Website sections
----------------
- Home
- About
- Courses
- Pricing
- Gallery
- Blog
- Contact
